2026 NFL Draft Class

The Jets maximized their draft capital with three immediate-impact starters and several depth pieces. Here's the full breakdown.

2

David Bailey, DE

Round 1 · Pick #2 · Texas Tech

Pros

  • Led FBS in sacks (14.5) and QB pressure rate (20.4%) in 2025
  • 4.5-sec 40-yard dash; elite get-off speed
  • First double-digit sack threat in a decade

Cons

  • Limited run defense (only 8 run stops in 2025)
  • Some analysts preferred Arvell Reese (Ohio State)
16

Kenyon Sadiq, TE

Round 1 · Pick #16 · Oregon

Pros

  • Rare physical traits: 4.39 40-yard dash, 43.5" vertical, 241 lbs
  • Can box out and outrun defenders
  • Adds explosiveness to a scoring-challenged offense

Cons

  • May be used more as a flex receiver than traditional TE
30

Omar Cooper Jr., WR

Round 1 · Pick #30 · Indiana

Pros

  • Mendoza's top target in 2025
  • Adds another playmaker to Wilson's WR group
  • Fits the Jets' offensive scheme perfectly

Cons

  • Could be a WR1 on other teams; may be underutilized as WR2
50

D'Angelo Ponds, CB

Round 2 · Pick #50 · Indiana

Pros

  • Fits coach Aaron Glenn's scheme well
  • Tone-setter on the back end despite small frame

Cons

  • Limited size and coverage range; may need scheme-specific usage
103

Darrell Jackson Jr., DT

Round 4 · Pick #103 · Florida State

Pros

  • Good value pick in the 4th round
  • Adds depth to an already loaded D-line

Cons

  • Not a top-tier pass rusher; primarily a depth contributor
110

Cade Klubnik, QB

Round 4 · Pick #110 · Clemson

Pros

  • Quick release, solid rhythm, dual-threat ability
  • 40+ college starts; could push Geno Smith

Cons

  • Decision-making under pressure still a question mark
  • 2025 regression hurt stock; no QB need at the time
188

Anez Cooper, G

Round 6 · Pick #188 · Miami

Pros

  • Adds offensive line depth
  • Good late-round value pick

Cons

  • Primarily a depth piece; starter only if injuries hit the O-line
228

VJ Payne, SAF

Round 7 · Pick #228 · Kansas State

Pros

  • Adds secondary depth
  • Fits the Jets' defensive scheme. Great size, hard hitter, would be shocked for him to start half way thru the season

Cons

  • 2nd String ; primarily a depth contributor right now

Overall Draft Grade

The Jets maximized their draft capital, landing three immediate-impact starters in Bailey, Sadiq, and Cooper, plus several quality depth pieces. Bailey is the biggest upgrade — the pass rush threat this organization has been missing for a decade. Sadiq and Cooper add offensive firepower around Garrett Wilson and Geno Smith. Klubnik was the riskiest pick but brings genuine upside if he develops quickly behind center.
